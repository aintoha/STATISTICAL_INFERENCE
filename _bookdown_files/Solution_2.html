\[\begin{align} P(V) =& \frac{P(V \mid M) P(M) + P(V \mid H) P(H)}{P(V)} \\ =& 0.9 \times 0.02 + 0.98 \times 0.2 = 0.214 \end{align}\]

  1. Let \(C_{i}\) represent the door where the car is hidden, and \(H_{j}\) represent the door that the host opens. You have already chosen Door 1.
  • Initially, the car is equally likely to be behind any of the three doors:

\[P(C_{1})=P(C_{2})=P(C_{3})=\frac{1}{3}\]

  • The host must open a door that you did not choose, and it must reveal a goat.

    • Case A: If the car is behind Door 1 \(C_{1}\)

    • The host can open either Door 2 or Door 3 with equal probability:

\[P(H_{3}\mid C_{1})=\frac{1}{2}\]

  • Case B: If the car is behind Door 2, \(C_{2}\)

  • The host cannot open Door 1 (your choice) and cannot open Door 2 (the car). He is forced to open Door 3:

\[P(H_{3}\mid C_{2})=1\]

  • Case C: If the car is behind Door 3, \(C_{3}\)

  • The host cannot open Door 3 because the car is there. Therefore, the probability he opens Door 3 is zero:

\[P(H_{3}\mid C_{3})=0\]

Total Probability of the Host Opening Door 3

We calculate the overall probability that the host opens Door 3 ((P(H_3))) by summing all possible scenarios:

\[\begin{align} P(H_{3})=&[P(H_{3}\mid C_{1})\cdot P(C_{1})]+[P(H_{3}\mid C_{2})\cdot P(C_{2})]+[P(H_{3}\mid C_{3})\cdot P(C_{3})] \\ P(H_{3})=&\left(\frac{1}{2}\cdot \frac{1}{3}\right)+\left(1\cdot \frac{1}{3}\right)+\left(0\cdot \frac{1}{3}\right) \\ P(H_{3})=&\frac{1}{6}+\frac{1}{3}+0=\frac{3}{6}=\frac{1}{2} \end{align}\]

Applying Bayes’ Theorem (The Posterior Probabilities)

Now we update our probabilities given the condition that the host has opened Door 3.

  • Chance of winning if you Stick (Door 1):

\[\begin{align} P(C_{1}\mid H_{3})=\frac{P(H_{3}\mid C_{1})\cdot P(C_{1})}{P(H_{3})}=\frac{\frac{1}{2}\cdot \frac{1}{3}}{\frac{1}{2}}=\frac{1}{3}\approx \mathbf{33.33\%} \end{align}\]

  • Chance of winning if you Switch (Door 2):

\[\begin{align} P(C_{2}\mid H_{3})=\frac{P(H_{3}\mid C_{2})\cdot P(C_{2})}{P(H_{3})}=\frac{1\cdot \frac{1}{3}}{\frac{1}{2}}=\frac{2}{3}\approx \mathbf{66.67\%} \end{align}\]
